How to install

  1. Tap Download the app and wait until the download finishes.
  2. Open the downloaded file and tap Install.
  3. If the phone asks, allow the browser to install apps ("Allow from this source"), then go back and tap Install.

If the app is already on the phone it is updated and its settings (receiver URL) are kept. After that, updates come from inside the app.
